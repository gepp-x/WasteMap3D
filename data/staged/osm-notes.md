## Method details

- **Waterway selection**: `waterway=river|canal|stream|drain|ditch` ways with `name`, `name:th` or `name:en`, plus all `waterway=river|canal` ways (named or not). Unnamed ways inherit a name only from a named `waterway` relation they are a member of (`nameSource=relation`). A way tagged only with a romanised `name` (no `name:th`) takes the Thai `name:th` that every other way with the identical `name` carries, if exactly one such value exists (`nameSource=sibling`, e.g. Hat Yai culvert way/243314050 'Klong Samsib Mate' -> คลองสามสิบเมตร). No names are inferred from geometry/proximity for centrelines; the only proximity-based naming is the Chao Phraya water-area (`nameSource=centreline`). Unnamed streams/drains/ditches are dropped ("tiny drains"). `ditch` is mapped to kind `drain`.
- **Merging**: ways with identical (kind, name) are line-merged at end nodes shared by exactly two pieces; pieces of the same name that touch at any node become one MultiLineString feature. Same-name canals that do not touch stay separate features. Unnamed ways are merged only end-to-end (never grouped).
- **widthClass** (display class, not a measured width): 3 = the city's principal river (Bangkok: name starts แม่น้ำเจ้าพระยา; Hat Yai: name starts คลองอู่ตะเภา or the tagged variant คลองอุ่ตะเภา), any `waterway=river` named แม่น้ำ… or '… River', and river water-areas; 2 = named khlong (name starts คลอง/ลำ/Khlong/Klong, any of waterway=river|canal|stream) whose same-name ways total ≥ 8 km (Bangkok) / ≥ 3 km (Hat Yai) inside the bbox after clipping, or that carry a `width` tag ≥ 10 m; canal water-areas; 1 = everything else, including unnamed ways. Many Bangkok khlongs are tagged `waterway=river` in OSM; they keep `kind: "river"` (the OSM tag) but get widthClass 2/1 unless they are the principal river.
- **Chao Phraya water area (Bangkok)**: candidates are `natural=water + water=river|riverbank` and legacy `waterway=riverbank` ways/multipolygons within 400 m of the `waterway=river` centreline named แม่น้ำเจ้าพระยา…, plus any `natural=water` element named *เจ้าพระยา*. Kept: parts named เจ้าพระยา / Chao Phraya, and unnamed parts that the centreline (returned by the same query/snapshot) actually intersects (turf.booleanIntersects). Parts named something else, and unnamed parts the centreline does not cross, are dropped (listed above). Kept parts are dissolved (mapshaper -dissolve2) into one feature, clipped to the bbox, and take name/name:en from the centreline (`nameSource=centreline`) when any part is unnamed.
- **Hat Yai water areas**: all `natural=water + water=river|riverbank|canal|stream` (and `waterway=riverbank`) polygons in the buffered bbox, grouped by name (unnamed parts stay separate, name=null).
- **Drainage selection**: `man_made=pumping_station`, `pumping_station=*`, `waterway=sluice_gate|lock_gate|weir|dam|floodgate|flood_gate`, `man_made=monitoring_station + monitoring:water_level=yes`, `basin~retention|detention`, plus Thai/English name patterns (`แก้มลิง|สถานีสูบน้ำ|บ่อสูบน้ำ|ประตูระบายน้ำ|บ่อหน่วงน้ำ|อุโมงค์ระบายน้ำ|วัดระดับน้ำ` / `pump(ing)? station|sluice|flood ?gate|retention pond|detention pond|monkey cheek|kaem ?ling|keam ?ling|water level station|drainage tunnel`). Name-only matches carrying POI keys (highway, public_transport, railway, shop, tourism, amenity, office, place, boundary, route, healthcare, craft, leisure, historic) are excluded. Water-supply pumping stations (`pumping_station=water`, names with ประปา/สูบจ่ายน้ำ/น้ำดิบ) are excluded. Sewage/wastewater pumping stations (`pumping_station=wastewater`, or Thai names containing สถานีสูบน้ำเสีย) are kept as `pump-station` exactly as tagged — they are part of the BMA drainage-and-sewerage system but are not stormwater pumps; inspect `tags`/`name` before treating a point as flood-drainage capacity.
- **Representative points**: nodes as-is; closed ways and multipolygons → turf centre of mass, or point-on-surface if the centre falls outside the shape; open ways → point at half the line length; other relations → mean of member vertices.
- **Simplification**: mapshaper 0.7 `-clip bbox=… -simplify dp interval=<m> keep-shapes -o precision=0.00001 rfc7946`.

## Caveats

- OSM snapshot dates differ between queries because different Overpass mirrors served them (spread 0.0 days): bkk-waterways = 2026-11-03T19:18:43Z (https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter); bkk-chaophraya-water = 2026-11-03T19:28:46Z (https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter); bkk-drainage = 2026-11-03T19:06:39Z (https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter); hy-waterways = 2026-11-03T18:52:33Z (https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter); hy-water-areas = 2026-11-03T18:52:33Z (https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter); hy-drainage = 2026-11-03T18:53:35Z (https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter). A mirror's database can lag the main OSM database by weeks or months; edits made after a snapshot date are not included.
- OSM is crowd-sourced and drainage infrastructure is sparsely mapped: the Bangkok bbox yields only pump-station=14, gate=78, retention=80, monitoring=1; the Hat Yai bbox yields gate=2, retention=1 (no pumping stations or sluice gates tagged). Bangkok's drainage system (BMA Drainage and Sewerage Department) is known to comprise far more pumping stations, floodgates and monkey-cheek (แก้มลิง) retention areas than this. Treat drainage points as *examples of mapped features*, never as counts, densities or coverage.
- 72 of the Bangkok `basin=retention` points fall inside the box 100.72–100.77 E, 13.64–13.72 N, i.e. the Suvarnabhumi Airport area (Bang Phli, Samut Prakan — outside the BMA boundary): they are the airport's individually-mapped retention ponds, which dominates the 'retention' count.
- Waterway names follow OSM tagging and are not corrected. Where a way has both a romanised `name` and a Thai `name:th` (e.g. Hat Yai name='Klong Ror Hok', name:th='คลอง ร6'), the output `name` is the Thai `name:th`; the romanised `name` is not copied into `nameEn` (nameEn = `name:en` only). Ways with only a romanised `name` keep it as `name` unless the sibling rule (Method details) resolves a Thai name. The Hat Yai downstream U-Taphao segment is tagged 'คลองอุ่ตะเภา' (sic, ุ่ instead of ู่) as waterway=stream, while the upstream segment is 'คลองอู่ตะเภา' waterway=river; unnamed waterway=river ways that join them end-to-end (e.g. way/502095133) are left unnamed.
- Many river/canal segments are unnamed in OSM; they are kept with `name: null` so the network is visually continuous, but they cannot be attributed to a specific khlong.
- `widthClass` is a display hint derived from tags/length, not a measured channel width or capacity.
- Clipping to the bbox cuts features at the bbox edge; the Bangkok bbox also covers parts of neighbouring provinces (Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan, Pathum Thani, Samut Sakhon).
