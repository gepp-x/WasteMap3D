## Method

1. **Anchor**: the OSM `amenity=townhall` whose `name`/`name:th`/`official_name`/`alt_name` contains `สำนักงานเขต<nameTh>` (whitespace-insensitive) and whose position lies inside the area polygon (node position, or Overpass `out center` = bounding-box centre for ways/relations). Ranking: exact name > prefix > contains; way > node > relation; lowest id. Otherwise `turf.pointOnFeature` (6 decimals) → `point-on-surface`.
2. **Window**: square of half-size 450 m around the anchor (lat ± 450/110574°, lon ± 450/(111320·cos lat0)°). One Overpass query per area: `way["building"]`, `relation["type"="multipolygon"]["building"]`, `way["highway"~"^(motorway|motorway_link|trunk|trunk_link|primary|primary_link|secondary|secondary_link|tertiary|tertiary_link|unclassified|residential)$"]`, `way["waterway"]`, natural=water / water=* / waterway=riverbank|dock / landuse=reservoir|basin ways and multipolygon relations; `out tags geom` (full member geometry).
3. **Local ENU metres**: x = (lon − lon0)·cos(lat0)·111320, y = (lat − lat0)·110574, rounded to 0.1 m; closing vertex dropped; consecutive duplicates removed; footprint and water rings normalised to counter-clockwise. Equirectangular approximation: error < 0.1 m within ±450 m at these latitudes.
4. **Buildings**: `building=no|none` skipped; ways must be closed; multipolygon relations keep only their largest assembled outer ring (inner courtyards ignored), id = −relationId. Kept when the polygon area centroid is inside the window (footprints may extend beyond it). At most 6000 per window — smallest footprints dropped first.
5. **Heights** (rounded to 0.1 m): numeric `height` (units m/ม./เมตร stripped, ft/' × 0.3048; accepted 0 < h ≤ 500 m) → `hs:"height"`; else first number of `building:levels` (0 < n ≤ 150) × 3.2 m → `hs:"levels"`; else deterministic **estimate** h = clamp(4 + 2.2·√A/6 + (FNV-1a-32(String(osmId)) mod 7), 4, 24) with A = footprint area m² → `hs:"estimated"`. Estimated heights are illustrative only and must not be presented as observed.
6. **Roads / water**: polylines clipped segment-wise (Liang–Barsky) to the window, polygons clipped with Sutherland–Hodgman (may leave zero-width slivers along the window edge for concave shapes). `t` = highway class / waterway value / water kind (`water` tag, else riverbank|dock|reservoir|basin, else "water"). Water-area inner rings (islands) are ignored.

## Caveats

- OSM building coverage in Thai cities is uneven: some blocks are fully traced (often from imagery imports), others are partly or entirely missing. Building counts are **not** a census and must not be used as a density statistic.
- Only a small share of buildings carry `height` or `building:levels`; the majority of heights are deterministic placeholders (`hs:"estimated"`) and are clamped to 4–24 m, so towers without tags are rendered far too low. The UI should label estimated heights.
- `building:levels` × 3.2 m ignores roof levels, podium/tower splits and `min_height`; `building:part` objects are not used.
- The window is a fixed 900 m × 900 m square in local metres around one anchor; it covers only a small part of each district and is not representative of the whole district.
- Roads exclude `service`, `living_street`, tracks, paths and footways; Bangkok sois tagged as `service` therefore do not appear.
- Overpass `out center` for a townhall way/relation is the bounding-box centre, which can sit a few tens of metres from the building itself.
- Water covers OSM waterways and water polygons only; the sea (OSM `natural=coastline`) is not included, and water polygons lose their islands (inner rings).
- `retrievedAt` is when the raw Overpass response was downloaded; the OSM data itself is as of the `timestamp_osm_base` recorded per area in the report. Windows fetched at different times can reflect slightly different OSM snapshots.
