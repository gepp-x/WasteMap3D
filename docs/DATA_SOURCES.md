# Data sources

Every number in the dashboard carries a `sourceId` that points into the machine-readable registry
`public/data/sources.json` (generated by `scripts/data/build-datasets.ts` from the staged registries
`data/staged/{bangkok,hatyai,rain,osm}-sources.json`, the building-window registries, and the
second-round registries `data/staged/{waste-gaps,hydrology,hatyai-community-locations}-sources.json`;
when an id appears in more than one registry the first entry is kept). 97 sources in total. This page is the human-readable companion: who published each source, where it is, its
licence, the period it covers, whether it was retrieved, and which outputs it feeds.

- **Retrieval:** the first round of live retrievals happened on **2026-11-03 (UTC)**; the second round
  (PCD generated waste, Hat Yai composition, community locations, water levels, BMA drainage inventory,
  Bangkok flood history) on **2026-11-04 (UTC)** (exact `retrievedAt` per source in `sources.json`; raw files in `data/raw/<source>/` with `.meta.json` sidecars). Re-running the
  fetchers with `--offline` rebuilds everything from those cached raw files.
- **Buddhist Era:** Gregorian year = BE − 543. A Thai fiscal year (FY, ปีงบประมาณ) runs 1 Oct – 30 Sep
  and is labelled with the Gregorian year in which it ends (FY BE 2569 = 2025-10-01 … 2026-09-30 → 2026).
- **Access status:** `retrieved` (downloaded and parsed), `partial` (only part usable), `failed`
  (attempted, not obtained), `manual-reference` (cited but not processed, or a project-internal entry).

Detailed retrieval logs, HTTP statuses, schemas and caveats per research stream:
`data/staged/bangkok-notes.md`, `data/staged/hatyai-notes.md`, `data/staged/rain-notes.md`,
`data/staged/osm-notes.md`, `data/staged/waste-gaps-notes.md`, `data/staged/hydrology-notes.md`,
`data/staged/hatyai-community-locations-notes.md`.
